Common questions

How do I cancel Pro?

On your iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Self Tape Pro > Cancel Subscription. Apple handles all billing.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Open Self Tape > Profile > Settings > Self Tape Pro > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where are my takes?

Inside the app on your iPhone, under each scene and in All takes on the home screen. We never keep a copy.
